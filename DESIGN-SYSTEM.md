# LSY Design System v1.0

Status: locked on 2026-08-12

## Brand roles

| Role | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | `#079ACB` | H1, icons, main button, key links |
| Accent | `#B91C2B` | Product-derived emphasis, never long body text |
| Canvas | `#FFFFFF` | Main reading surface |
| Alternate surface | `#F7F7F8` | Section rhythm and form background |
| Ink | `#111111` | Main text |
| Muted ink | `#5B5B5B` | Supporting text |

The palette is extracted from the original LSY droplet mark and the red product cabinet. Purple, neon and unrelated gradients are outside the system.

## Typography and geometry

- Profile: `swiss`.
- Display and body: Inter with system sans-serif fallback.
- H1 is the single dominant statement. Section headings stay left-aligned unless the chassis intentionally centers a decision moment.
- Radius: 2px for content surfaces. Pill geometry is reserved for actions and compact tags already defined by the chassis.
- Depth: thin border and near-flat shadow. No glass blur or colored glow.

## Layout

- Maximum content width follows the shared chassis, 1152px for section grids and 768px for long reading.
- Section order follows buyer decision flow: category, process, entity, questions, visual decision, contact.
- One surface has one task. The product visual appears once as the main art moment.

## Signature

Signature scene: `Clear-water kitchen`. A recognizable LSY purifier sits in natural morning light with quiet aqua water reflections and enough clear space for the decision copy.

Signature interaction: the existing short button press feedback. No additional looping animation is introduced.

## Asset truth

- `public/logo.png`: source brand asset from the LSY pairing application.
- `public/logo.svg`: lightweight vector fallback based on the same droplet and wordmark.
- `public/hero-lsy.png`: ImageGen product scene derived from the public LSY product reference.
- `public/og.png`: social preview using the same approved product scene.

## Prohibited

- Do not add health outcomes, certification seals or product specifications to imagery without an owner-supplied source.
- Do not replace the aqua and red pair with a generic technology palette.
- Do not add decorative particles, glow, glass cards, fake testimonials or unsupported statistics.
- New visual decisions require a new version and an updated art handoff.
