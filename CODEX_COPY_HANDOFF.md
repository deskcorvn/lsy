# CODEX_COPY_HANDOFF: LSY landing, 2026-08-12

## Context

The scaffold contained generic agency copy. The new landing needs to identify the product category, explain what happens before installation and lead a household buyer to the contact form. Source brief: `.agents/product-marketing.md`.

## Decision 1: Homepage H1

- A. `Máy lọc nước LSY`. The shortest accurate category and brand statement; the household context moves to the subheading so the product image stays unobstructed.
- B. `Chọn máy lọc nước LSY theo nhu cầu sử dụng`. More action-oriented, but slightly less immediate as a brand statement.
- C. `Biết rõ phương án trước khi lắp máy lọc nước`. Strong purchase tension, but LSY appears later than desired.

Codex decision: approve A.

## Decision 2: Main positioning

- A. Ask about source water, household size and installation position, then present a clear option before the buyer decides.
- B. Lead with connected monitoring and present the purifier as a smart-home device.
- C. Lead with the company history and local support channel.

Codex decision: approve A. Monitoring remains supporting proof because the software exists locally, while detailed product hardware specifications are not yet verified.

## Decision 3: Primary CTA

- A. `Nhận tư vấn máy phù hợp`.
- B. `Gửi nhu cầu cho LSY`.
- C. `Để LSY gọi lại`.

Codex decision: use A in the hero and B at the product visual. Both name the immediate result and point to the same form.

## Constraints

- Address readers as `bạn` throughout.
- No price, warranty period, filtration performance, medical benefit, certification or customer count without an owner-supplied source.
- Use the company and product facts listed in `.agents/product-marketing.md`.
- The Facebook URL and public phone are allowed. The domain email remains an operational provisioning item.
