# CODEX_ART_HANDOFF: LSY landing, 2026-08-12

## Ready state

- Primary action: submit the consultation form.
- Audience: household buyer comparing water purifiers.
- North-star scene: a clear family kitchen in morning light.
- Brand traits: clean, factual, approachable.
- Asset truth: original LSY logo, public product reference and one generated product scene.
- Responsive targets: 390, 768 and 1440 pixels.
- Motion: no new decorative animation.

## Decision 1: Palette

- A. White canvas, LSY aqua primary and deep red product accent.
- B. White canvas, LSY aqua primary and dark navy accent.
- C. Warm neutral canvas with aqua used sparingly.

Codex decision: approve A because both strong colors are extracted from the supplied LSY assets. Red is limited to emphasis so it does not compete with the blue identity.

## Decision 2: Main visual

- A. Reference-based LSY purifier in a bright, ordinary family kitchen.
- B. Isolated product cutout on a clinical white studio background.
- C. Abstract droplet and water-ripple illustration without a product.

Codex decision: approve A. It shows the actual purchase category and gives the product a credible household context. The visual contains no added specification, certification or medical claim.

## Decision 3: Signature

- A. `Clear-water kitchen`: quiet water reflections, strong white space and one product focal point.
- B. `Droplet frame`: repeat the logo silhouette around content cards.
- C. `Connected dashboard`: use device telemetry as the main visual language.

Codex decision: approve A. It is specific to LSY without turning the homepage into a dashboard product page.

## Design humanizer scan

The selected direction avoids purple gradients, glassmorphism, glow, fake statistics, stock people, anonymous testimonials and decorative emoji. The shared chassis still provides a three-card process row, but the rest of the page changes rhythm with prose, FAQ, a full-width product scene and contact surfaces. No `src/` change is introduced.
